Da Nang is in central Vietnam and combines miles of tropical coastline, limestone mountains, mystical stories, local culture and modern infrastructure. Its location makes it the perfect starting point for exploring nearby places like Hoi An, Ba Na Hills and the former imperial capital of Hue.

This guide is designed to make the most of a **3-day itinerary in Da Nang**, taking into account the logistics of getting around, the must-do activities and the connection with local culture.

## Weather and Best Time to Visit Da Nang

Da Nang's climate is warm all year round, but it has two clearly distinct seasons:

* **Dry season (January to August):** Days are usually sunny and hot, the water at My Khe looks clear and the weather at Ba Na Hills is clearer. According to tourism guides, the best window is February to May, with mild, stable weather. June to August is hotter and is the peak season for local tourism.
* **Rainy season (September to December):** Marked by frequent rain and rough seas, with October and November the months most likely to bring heavy rain or typhoons on the central coast.

We were there in August and September. August had little rain and some really good beach days; September, on the other hand, was very rainy, almost every day and sometimes all day long.

Keep in mind that these dates are approximate and vary depending on the source. The transition months (August-September and December-January) change from year to year, and with the climate becoming more unpredictable, seasons sometimes start earlier, start later or run longer or shorter. Even in the middle of the rainy season it doesn't rain every day, so it's worth checking the forecast before heading out.

## Day 1: Son Tra Peninsula, the Beach and the Dragon Bridge

### Morning: Son Tra Peninsula, the Pagoda and Lady Buddha

The best way to start is early in the morning, heading to the **Son Tra Peninsula**, the green strip that wraps around Da Nang Bay. This is where you'll find **Linh Ung Bãi Bụt Pagoda**, an active pagoda looked after by the local community and resident monks. The building stands out for its golden details and dragon sculptures, and it offers a privileged panoramic view of the whole bay. On the grounds stands the statue of **Lady Buddha (Quan Am)**, 67 meters tall and facing the city; you can also visit the pagoda and the beautiful garden (cover photo).

> 💡 Is Lady Buddha one of the three vertices of the spiritual triangle that protects the city? There is a local legend that links faith with the typhoon weather data on the coast. You can read the full story in our article **[The Sacred Triangle That Protects the City of Da Nang](https://blogcito.com/en/journal/sacred-triangle-da-nang-linh-ung-pagodas/)**

### Afternoon: My Khe Beach and a Stroll Around An Thuong

Staying in the **An Thuong** neighborhood makes it easy to walk to **My Khe Beach**, one of the longest urban beaches in Asia.

It's a beautiful beach of golden sand and warm water, lined with palm trees and sheltered by the mountains, with the figure of Lady Buddha in the distance. On the beach you can rent sun loungers with straw umbrellas, buy fresh coconuts or enjoy a massage by the sea. Just one block away is the tourist area, perfect for lunch (local and international options), fruit juices or browsing local shops.

<div class="post-img-box img-completo img-centro"><img src="/img/diario/playa-my-khe-beach-.webp" alt="Imagen de bitácora"><span class="img-caption">My Khe Beach, Da Nang</span></div>

### Evening: Son Tra Night Market and the Dragon Bridge Show

At sunset, we suggest heading to the **Dragon Bridge (Cau Rong)**. Just steps from the bridge is the **Son Tra Night Market**, great for trying local seafood skewers, vegetarian options and fresh juices. The market is quick to walk through, with souvenirs, coffee, crafts and clothing. Although we recommend going on the weekend (Friday to Sunday) to catch the bridge show, the market gets a lot busier on those days, especially during the summer high season (June to August).

* **The not-to-miss moment:** If your visit falls on a **Friday, Saturday or Sunday at 21:00**, traffic on the bridge is stopped for the famous show in which the dragon's head breathes fire and water. The show runs until 21:30. It's best to arrive about 15 minutes early to get a good spot along the Han River. You can even stand under the water spout to experience it up close.

## Day 2: Marble Mountains and the Magic of Hoi An

### Morning: A Beach Start and the Marble Mountains

Morning is an ideal time to enjoy My Khe Beach: fewer crowds, the water looks clear and there's nothing better than starting with a morning swim. *Tip:* The earlier you go, the gentler the sun. In peak season, by 10:30 the radiation already feels very strong. For those who enjoy sunrises, watching the sun come up in the calm of My Khe is 100% recommended.

After a good brunch, we recommend heading to the **Marble Mountains (Ngu Hanh Son)**. This group of five limestone hills holds deep caves turned into Buddhist and Hindu shrines, with great historical and spiritual value.

* **Highlight:** The **Huyen Khong** cave, where rays of light filter through openings in the rocky ceiling, lighting up ancient altars. Exploring the whole complex takes around 1.5 to 2 hours.

<div class="post-img-box img-completo img-centro"><img src="/img/diario/marble-mountains-temple-statue-cave-praying.webp" alt="Imagen de bitácora"><span class="img-caption">Marble Mountains cave in Da Nang</span></div>

### Afternoon and Evening: Day Trip to Hoi An and a Handmade Lantern Workshop

After lunch, you can take a Grab or a taxi to the historic city of **Hoi An** (just 45 minutes from Da Nang).

1. **Walk Through the Old Town:** Stroll among yellow facades of Chinese, Japanese and French merchant architecture, declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
2. **[Lantern Making Workshop](https://www.getyourguide.com/hoi-an-l831/hoi-an-taller-de-elaboracion-de-farolillos-con-monitor-local-t1470965/?ranking_uuid=55bfa15c-c1c5-42db-951d-764132dda079&referral_redirect=1&q=H%E1%BB%99i+An):** A lovely cultural activity in the old town of Hoi An is the handmade lantern workshop. In about an hour, local artisans teach you how to build the bamboo frame and cover it with traditional silk. You take your own finished lantern home as a souvenir of the trip.
3. **Dusk and Wishes on the Hoai River:** When the sun sets, the old town is completely transformed, lit up by thousands of silk lanterns. You can take a [ride on a small traditional wooden boat and make a wish by releasing a floating candle on the river](https://www.getyourguide.com/hoi-an-l831/hoi-an-paseo-nocturno-en-barco-por-el-rio-hoai-y-linterna-flotante-t571429/?ranking_uuid=e92057bf-0422-4a48-bbce-4b0f8c6826e7&q=hoi+an+barco+linterna&adults=1).

> 💡 **Extra tip in Hoi An:** If you have extra time in the afternoon, you can add a [**local cooking class** to learn how to make *Cao Lầu and other typical dishes*](https://www.getyourguide.com/hoi-an-l831/hoi-an-clase-de-cocina-tradicional-y-comida-con-una-familia-local-t562488?ranking_uuid=244dfd89-1d5c-4d13-9bc6-9c50dd91e9b1&q=clase+de+cocina+en+hoi+an&date_from=2026-09-25&date_to=2026-09-25&adults=1&_pc=1,1) or visit the famous **artisan tailors** in the historic center, known for making custom suits and dresses in 24 hours.

## Day 3: Ba Na Hills, Markets and a Night in the City

### Morning: Going Up to Ba Na Hills and the Golden Bridge

A must in Da Nang is, without a doubt, Ba Na Hills, a theme park at the top of the mountain, 1,500 meters above sea level. You get there on one of the longest single-cable cable car systems in the world, which crosses forests and waterfalls. We recommend spending the first hours of the day on the way up, to avoid the crowds or the fog during the rainy season.

### What to See at Ba Na Hills:

* **The icon:** The **Golden Bridge**, held up in the air by two giant stone hands.
* **French Village and the Moon Castle:** A reconstruction of a medieval European village with cobblestone streets, churches and squares. A buffet lunch is included in the entrance fee.
* **Amusement park:** It includes arcade games, free-fall towers, 5D simulators and themed areas inspired by Jules Verne's novels.
  * There is also an Alpine Coaster (mountain sled) track that takes you down the slope with views of the forest.
* **Tip:** Leave Da Nang at 07:00 to board the cable car at 08:00. This lets you walk across the bridge before the tour groups arrive.
* *Note:* It's always a good idea to check the weather forecast the day before, since with rain or dense fog visibility drops quite a bit.

### Afternoon: Exploring Local Markets and Traditional Cafés

Back in downtown Da Nang, you can wander through the local markets to buy coffee, souvenirs or clothes:

* **Han Market (Chợ Hàn):** The most famous market for buying souvenirs. Prices tend to be affordable, and the stalls range from local coffee and snacks to made-to-measure clothing. It's a covered market facing the Han River, perfect to visit while you stroll along the riverbank.
* **Con Market (Chợ Cồn):** A 100% authentic, less touristy experience. Its food court serves typical dishes like ***Mì Quảng*** (rice noodles in a short, concentrated broth with peanuts and fresh herbs) or ***Bánh Xèo*** (crispy rice-flour and turmeric crepes filled with sprouts and vegetables) at local prices.
* **Coffee break:** A few blocks from the shopping area you'll find lovely traditional cafés that serve the classic Vietnamese egg coffee (also in a cocoa version), along with fresh baked goods in a calm, cozy atmosphere.

  <div class="post-img-box img-mediano img-centro"><img src="/img/diario/café-con-huevo-egg-coffe.webp" alt="Imagen de bitácora"><span class="img-caption">Egg coffee with condensed milk, a typical Vietnamese drink, highly recommended</span></div>

### Evening: Dinner at a Beach Club or an Urban Night at Helio Night Market

For your last night in the city, you can choose the plan that best suits your travel style:

* **Option A (Relax & Sea):** Have dinner by the sea at a restaurant or beach club in the My Khe area. Many of these places have live DJs and a pleasant atmosphere right next to the beach.
* **Option B (Urban & Food Scene):** Visit the **Helio Night Market**, a modern night market with outdoor food stalls and live music. Or pick a local restaurant: if it has little plastic stools on the sidewalk and good Google reviews, it's the right place.
